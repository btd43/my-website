Brandon Tyler Dalton

brandon@fewture.co

I’m building Internet Racing League, a new sports league set out to reshape grassroots motorsports. My work sits at the intersection of sport, media, and culture, building new platforms around emerging communities.

  • In mid 2015, I came to Los Angeles with Shane and Jeremy Gonzales, where I would meet some of the most important artists and creatives of the past decade.
  • In mid 2019, graduated with a B.A. in Business Management from CSUSM.
  • In late 2019, I joined FaZe Clan, living in the houses with the talent. I quickly found myself in a unique position in the business, sitting between executives and founding talent. I became one of the most trusted voices in the building on brand direction, strategy, and recruitment. I pushed hard on bringing in Ronaldo. I hired Gab3 to FaZe, in pursuit of extending the lifespan of the brand and reimagining what the future of livestreaming and content creation could look and feel like. I became acquainted with the biggest talent in the space and had a front row seat to the reasons the business didn't scale.
  • In late 2020, after a road trip with friends, I conceptualized a world where motocross and fashion could come together organically for the first time in decades—I called it 4ortythre3, a project where I recontextualized Trevor Stewart's career as a professional motocross racer into something more hybrid and expression based. I led all direction, production, styling, editing, and curating for his brand from 2020-2024. In that time our work caught the attention of people like Playboi Carti, Kanye West, Matthew Williams and more, as well as working with brands like Nike, Yeezy, 424 and more.
  • In early 2023, I left FaZe to build Fewture Studios with Kai Henry—a studio focused on building and owning IP with big creators as partners.
  • In early 2023, Ken Carson released Suicidal on Lost Files 4—a song I produced. It was my first time a song I produced was released.
  • In early 2025, I founded and conceptualized Internet Racing League. I’m currently building the league with Jon Vlogs, one of the largest streamers in South America, and TMG—the holding company behind Neymar and Andre Akkari that owns and operates Furia.
  • In mid 2025, became a partner at Fewture Fund. A venture fund focused on investing in the attention economy.
  • In early 2026, on Senna Day in São Paulo at Interlagos, we had our first IRL race to 50,000 live viewers and kicked off the concept of the league live.
  • In mid 2026, Sheck Wes performed a song I solo produced called DDD in Romania to thousands.
