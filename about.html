About Brandon Dalton

brandon@fewture.co

Brandon Tyler Dalton is a Los Angeles–based founder, creative executive, and music producer. He is the founder of Internet Racing League (IRL), a creator-powered motorsports league, and previously worked in talent recruitment and brand development at FaZe Clan (2019–2023).

  • Internet Racing League (2025–) — Founder and conceptualizer. Global, creator-owned motorsport league bridging racing and entertainment, in partnership with TMG / FURIA ecosystem operators and creators including Jon Vlogs.
  • Fewture Studios / Fewture Fund (2023–) — Founder / Chief Attention Officer building creator-led IP; partner investing in the attention economy.
  • 4ortythre3 (2020–2024) — Founder / Creative Director. Motocross × fashion × music project with Trevor Stewart; work reached Carti, Kanye West, Matthew Williams and brands including Nike, Yeezy, and 424.
  • FaZe Clan (2019–2023) — Talent recruitment and brand development; lived in the houses with talent; trusted voice on brand direction and recruitment (including Stable Ronaldo / Gab3 era work). Featured in GQ.
  • Music — Producer credits including Ken Carson (“Suicidal”), Homixide Gang (“ADHD”), Sheck Wes (“DDD”), plus commercial music for Disney | FaZe Clan and Nike | Stussy. Credits on Genius.
  • Education — B.A. Business Management, California State University San Marcos (2019).
