I am currently working on Internet Racing League.

  • In 2015, I came to Los Angeles with Shane and Jeremy Gonzales, where I would meet some of the most important artists and creatives of the past decade.
  • In 2019, graduated with a B.A. in Business Management from CSUSM.
  • In 2019, I joined FaZe Clan, living in the houses with the talent and helping with overall strategy, talent recruitment, etc.
  • In 2020, after a road trip with friends, I started building a world where fashion and motocross collided—4ortythre3 with Trevor Stewart. The work that followed took us to Carti, Ye, Nike, and many more.
  • In 2023, I left FaZe to build Fewture Studios. Modular studio powering creator-led companies, media, and IP.
  • In 2023, Ken Carson released Suicidal on Lost Files 4—a song I produced that became a cult classic in that world.
  • In 2024, became a partner at Fewture Fund. Investing in creators as founders across sports, media, and culture.
  • In 2025, founded Internet Racing League. Global, creator-owned motorsport league bridging racing and entertainment.
