Brandon Dalton

brandon@fewture.co

I’m a co-founder of Internet Racing League, a motorsport league that brings live streamers and local driving talent from around the world to one track.

  • In 2015 I started going to Los Angeles with Shane & Jeremy Gonzales, finding myself around artists and creatives who ended up shaping the decade.
  • In 2019 I joined FaZe Clan, living in the houses with the talent and sitting between the executives and the creators. I pushed hard on recruiting the next biggest live streamers while bringing art, fashion, and music closer to the core of FaZe and gaming as a whole. I hired Gab3 and recruited Stable Ronaldo in my time there.
  • I also saw up close why the business did not scale properly.
  • In 2020 I started 4ortythre3 with Trevor Stewart, bringing motocross and fashion together for the first time in decades. Four years of direction, ideation, filming, editing, and styling that led to work with Playboi Carti, Kanye West, Matthew Williams, Nike, Yeezy & more.
  • In 2023 I left FaZe to build Fewture with Kai Henry.
  • That same year Ken Carson released Suicidal, the first song I produced to come out.
  • In late 2024 I conceptualized and founded Internet Racing League.
  • In 2025 I became a founding partner at Fewture Fund with Erik Moore, a venture fund investing in the attention economy.
  • In May 2026 we ran the first IRL race on Senna Day in São Paulo, Brazil at Interlagos. Bia Senna and Jon Vlogs streamed the day to 50,000 people watching live at once.
  • In mid 2026 Sheck Wes performed DDD, a song I produced, to thousands in Romania. I soon got to see it performed at Petco Park in San Diego, ten minutes from where I was born.
